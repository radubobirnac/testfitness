Product Requirements Document (PRD)
1. Executive Summary
StrengthWave is an intelligent fitness application that serves as a personalized strength coach in users' pockets. Using adaptive algorithms and scientific training principles, it creates customized workout programs that adjust based on user performance, equipment availability, and physical limitations. The app delivers the benefits of personalized training at a fraction of the cost of a human personal trainer while providing scientifically-backed programming that exceeds the quality of many in-person trainers.
The application will launch in two phases: a foundation phase establishing core functionality, and an enhanced intelligence phase that introduces advanced personalization and adaptive features. This phased approach allows for rapid initial deployment while ensuring a robust, data-driven evolution of the product.
2. Product Vision & Goals
2.1 Vision Statement
To democratize access to high-quality strength training by creating an adaptive, intelligent fitness coach that evolves with users, prevents injuries, and delivers optimal results based on scientific training principles.
2.2 Core Goals
Replace Personal Trainers: Provide personalized guidance that matches or exceeds the quality of in-person trainers at a fraction of the cost
Optimize Training: Implement scientific principles including proper volume, progression, and periodization
Ensure Safety: Accommodate injuries and limitations while preventing new ones through appropriate exercise selection
Enhance Consistency: Encourage regular training through intelligent programming and simplified workout execution
Democratize Knowledge: Make advanced training concepts like periodization accessible to all fitness levels
2.3 Key Differentiators
Adaptive Intelligence: Workouts that evolve based on user performance, not static programs
Scientific Foundation: Volume recommendations based on research (Jeff Nippard guidelines) rather than arbitrary programming
Personalized Periodization: Proper training cycles tailored to experience level (linear, undulating, or block)
Safety-First Approach: Comprehensive injury accommodation and prevention
Gender-Specific Focus: Training prioritization based on typical gender preferences while supporting full-body development
Seamless Offline Experience: Full functionality in gym environments without connectivity

4. Market Positioning & Value Proposition
4.1 Problem Statement
Personal trainers are prohibitively expensive ($100-$200+ per session) and often deliver suboptimal programming based on outdated principles. Generic workout apps offer cookie-cutter programs that don't adapt to user progress, available equipment, or physical limitations. Neither solution properly implements scientific training principles like appropriate volume, progressive overload, and periodization.

StrengthWave delivers personalized strength coaching that:
Costs a fraction of personal training while delivering superior programming based on scientific principles

Implements proper periodization to optimize results and prevent plateaus
Accommodates injuries and limitations to ensure safe, continuous progress

Tracks progress comprehensively to show tangible results and motivate consistency

5. Feature Specifications
5.1 Intelligent Workout Programming
5.1.1 Workout Generation Algorithm
Functionality: Creates personalized workouts based on multiple factors
Key Inputs:
User profile (experience, gender, goals)
Available equipment
Workout history and performance
Active injuries or limitations
Position in periodization cycle
Available time
Implementation Details:
Selects exercises based on weighted scoring system considering:
Gender relevance (upper body focus for men, lower body focus for women)
User preference (based on history and explicit feedback)
Equipment availability (with substitution logic)
Injury risk (avoiding exercises that stress injured areas)
Exercise variety (preventing staleness)
Movement pattern balance (push/pull/hinge/squat)
Assigns appropriate volume based on Jeff Nippard's guidelines:
Beginner: Lower range (e.g., 10 weekly sets for chest)
Intermediate: Middle range (e.g., 10-15 weekly sets for chest)
Advanced: Upper range (e.g., 15-20 weekly sets for chest)
Structures workouts with intelligent exercise ordering:
Compound movements before isolation
Non-competing supersets where appropriate
Proper rest intervals based on exercise intensity
User Experience:
One-tap workout generation based on user profile
Option to customize generated workouts
Ability to save favorite workouts for reuse
Clear explanation of workout focus and structure
5.1.2 Working Weight Detection & Progression
Functionality: Determines appropriate weights and progression for each user and exercise
Initial Weight Estimation:
Based on body weight percentages (5-20% depending on exercise)
Adjusted for gender (different multipliers for upper/lower body)
Modified by experience level (0.7× for beginners, 1.0× for intermediate, 1.3× for advanced)
Conservative for beginners (safety factor of 0.8-0.9)
Rounded to available weight increments (2.5lb/5lb)
Progression System:
Progressive overload implementation following scientific principles
Weight increases after completing target reps for 2 consecutive workouts
Percentage-based increases (typically 2.5-5%)
Different progression rates for compound vs. isolation exercises
Confidence scoring (0-100) to determine reliability of working weights
Weight reduction after repeated failures to complete target reps
User Experience:
Clear weight recommendations for each exercise
Simple feedback mechanism (too easy/appropriate/too hard)
Visual indicators of progress over time
Celebrations for new personal records
5.1.3 Periodization System
Functionality: Implements scientifically-backed training cycles to optimize results
Periodization Models:
Linear periodization for beginners (simplest implementation)
Undulating periodization for intermediate/advanced (better for hypertrophy)
Block periodization for very advanced users (specialized goals)
Cycle Structure:
Accumulation phase (higher reps, moderate weights, more volume)
Intensification phase (lower reps, heavier weights, less volume)
Deload phase (moderate reps, lighter weights, reduced volume)
Phase Transitions:
Time-based triggers (standard 4-week accumulation, 2-week intensification, 1-week deload)
Performance-based triggers (detecting plateaus)
User-initiated transitions (managing fatigue)
User Experience:
Visual indication of current phase and position in cycle
Educational content explaining periodization benefits
Automatic workout adjustments based on current phase
Option for user to initiate deload when feeling fatigued

Secondary muscle involvement (moderate impact)
Joint stress (high impact)
Movement patterns (relevant to specific injuries)

Low risk: Safe to perform normally
Medium risk: Perform with reduced weight
High risk: Substitute with safer alternative
Exercise Substitution Logic:
Identifies safer alternatives targeting same muscle groups
Prioritizes exercises with similar training effects
Adapts to available equipment
User Experience:

2.2 Key Tables Definition
2.2.1 Users & Profiles
CREATE TABLE users (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  auth_id TEXT UNIQUE NOT NULL,
  email TEXT UNIQUE NOT NULL,
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

CREATE TABLE user_profiles (
  user_id UUID PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  height NUMERIC,
  weight NUMERIC,
  gender TEXT CHECK (gender IN ('male', 'female', 'other', 'prefer_not_to_say')),
  experience_level TEXT CHECK (experience_level IN ('beginner', 'intermediate', 'advanced')),
  goal TEXT CHECK (goal IN ('strength', 'hypertrophy', 'endurance', 'general_fitness')),
  workout_days_per_week INTEGER DEFAULT 3,
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

2.2.2 Exercises & Muscles
CREATE TABLE exercises (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  name TEXT NOT NULL,
  description TEXT,
  instructions TEXT,
  media_url TEXT,
  equipment_required TEXT[] NOT NULL,
  difficulty TEXT CHECK (difficulty IN ('beginner', 'intermediate', 'advanced')),
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

CREATE TABLE muscles (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  name TEXT NOT NULL,
  body_region TEXT NOT NULL,
  parent_id UUID REFERENCES muscles(id) NULL
);

CREATE TABLE exercise_muscles (
  exercise_id UUID REFERENCES exercises(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  muscle_id UUID REFERENCES muscles(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  is_primary BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
  intensity INTEGER CHECK (intensity BETWEEN 1 AND 10),
  PRIMARY KEY (exercise_id, muscle_id)
);

CREATE TABLE tags (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  name TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE,
  category TEXT
);

CREATE TABLE exercise_tags (
  exercise_id UUID REFERENCES exercises(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  tag_id UUID REFERENCES tags(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  PRIMARY KEY (exercise_id, tag_id)
);

2.2.3 Workouts & Logging
CREATE TABLE workouts (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  name TEXT NOT NULL,
  description TEXT,
  is_template BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
  is_ai_generated BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
  focus_areas TEXT[],
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

CREATE TABLE workout_exercises (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  workout_id UUID REFERENCES workouts(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  exercise_id UUID REFERENCES exercises(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  order_index INTEGER NOT NULL,
  sets_target INTEGER NOT NULL,
  reps_target INTEGER NOT NULL,
  weight_target NUMERIC NULL,
  rest_seconds INTEGER DEFAULT 90
);

CREATE TABLE workout_logs (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  workout_id UUID REFERENCES workouts(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  started_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL,
  completed_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NULL,
  rating INTEGER CHECK (rating BETWEEN 1 AND 10),
  notes TEXT,
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

CREATE TABLE exercise_logs (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  workout_log_id UUID REFERENCES workout_logs(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  exercise_id UUID REFERENCES exercises(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  set_number INTEGER NOT NULL,
  weight_used NUMERIC NULL,
  reps_completed INTEGER NULL,
  is_completed BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
  feedback TEXT CHECK (feedback IN ('too_easy', 'appropriate', 'too_hard')),
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

2.2.4 Training Intelligence
CREATE TABLE injury_logs (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  body_part TEXT NOT NULL,
  pain_level INTEGER CHECK (pain_level BETWEEN 1 AND 10),
  notes TEXT,
  is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
  reported_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  resolved_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NULL,
  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

CREATE TABLE periodization_cycles (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  current_phase TEXT CHECK (current_phase IN ('accumulation', 'intensification', 'deload')),
  phase_week INTEGER DEFAULT 1,
  total_cycle_count INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
  started_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

3.1 Working Weight Detection
The system will use a progressive weight detection algorithm that balances safety with efficiency:
function calculateInitialWeight(user, exercise) {
  // Base percentages by exercise type
  const basePercentages = {
    'bench_press': 0.5,   // 50% of body weight
    'squat': 0.6,         // 60% of body weight
    'deadlift': 0.75,     // 75% of body weight 
    'overhead_press': 0.3,// 30% of body weight
    'bicep_curl': 0.15,   // 15% of body weight
    // Default for other exercises
    'default': 0.2        // 20% of body weight
  };
  
  // Gender multiplier (for upper/lower body differences)
  const genderMultiplier = {
    'male': {
      'upper_body': 1.0,
      'lower_body': 1.0
    },
    'female': {
      'upper_body': 0.7,
      'lower_body': 0.9
    }
  };
  
  // Experience multiplier
  const experienceMultiplier = {
    'beginner': 0.7,
    'intermediate': 1.0,
    'advanced': 1.3
  };
  
  // Safety factor
  const safetyFactor = 0.9;
  
  // Get base percentage (default if not specifically defined)
  const basePercentage = basePercentages[exercise.id] || basePercentages.default;
  
  // Determine body region (simplified)
  const bodyRegion = isPrimaryMuscleUpperBody(exercise) ? 'upper_body' : 'lower_body';
  
  // Calculate initial weight
  let initialWeight = user.weight * basePercentage;
  initialWeight *= genderMultiplier[user.gender][bodyRegion];
  initialWeight *= experienceMultiplier[user.experience_level];
  initialWeight *= safetyFactor;
  
  // Round to nearest weight increment (2.5 for small weights, 5 for larger)
  const increment = initialWeight < 20 ? 2.5 : 5;
  initialWeight = Math.floor(initialWeight / increment) * increment;
  
  return initialWeight;
}

3.2 Workout Generation Algorithm
The workout generation will use a modular approach to create personalized workouts:
async function generateWorkout(userId, availableTime, availableEquipment) {
  // Step 1: Gather user data
  const user = await getUserProfile(userId);
  const workoutHistory = await getRecentWorkouts(userId, 10);
  const activeInjuries = await getActiveInjuries(userId);
  const workingWeights = await getWorkingWeights(userId);
  const periodizationCycle = await getPeriodizationCycle(userId);
  
  // Step 2: Determine what to train today based on split and history
  const muscleGroups = determineTargetMuscleGroups(user, workoutHistory);
  
  // Step 3: Select exercises for each muscle group
  let exercises = [];
  for (const muscleGroup of muscleGroups) {
    const muscleExercises = await selectExercisesForMuscleGroup(
      muscleGroup, 
      user.experience_level,
      activeInjuries,
      availableEquipment,
      workoutHistory
    );
    exercises = [...exercises, ...muscleExercises];
  }
  
  // Step 4: Assign sets, reps, and weights based on periodization
  exercises = assignSetsAndReps(exercises, periodizationCycle, user.experience_level);
  exercises = assignWeights(exercises, workingWeights, periodizationCycle);
  
  // Step 5: Optimize for available time
  if (availableTime) {
    exercises = optimizeForTime(exercises, availableTime);
  }
  
  // Step 6: Create the workout
  const workout = {
    user_id: userId,
    name: generateWorkoutName(muscleGroups),
    description: generateWorkoutDescription(muscleGroups, periodizationCycle),
    is_template: false,
    is_ai_generated: true,
    focus_areas: muscleGroups,
    exercises: exercises
  };
  
  return workout;
}

3.3 Exercise Selection Algorithm
async function selectExercisesForMuscleGroup(
  muscleGroup, 
  experienceLevel, 
  activeInjuries,
  availableEquipment,
  workoutHistory
) {
  // Query database for suitable exercises
  const candidateExercises = await getExercisesForMuscleGroup(
    muscleGroup, 
    availableEquipment
  );
  
  // Calculate a score for each exercise
  const scoredExercises = candidateExercises.map(exercise => {
    const scores = {
      // Higher for gender-specific priorities
      genderRelevance: calculateGenderRelevance(exercise, user.gender),
      
      // Higher for exercises the user prefers
      userPreference: calculateUserPreference(exercise, workoutHistory),
      
      // Higher for exercises that haven't been done recently
      variety: calculateVarietyScore(exercise, workoutHistory),
      
      // Lower for exercises that stress injured areas
      injuryRisk: calculateInjuryRisk(exercise, activeInjuries),
      
      // Higher for exercises appropriate to experience level
      experienceMatch: calculateExperienceMatch(exercise, experienceLevel),
      
      // Higher for compound movements for beginners, more isolation for advanced
      movementType: calculateMovementTypeScore(exercise, experienceLevel)
    };
    
    // Calculate weighted total score
    const weights = getScoreWeights(experienceLevel);
    const totalScore = (
      (scores.genderRelevance * weights.genderRelevance) +
      (scores.userPreference * weights.userPreference) +
      (scores.variety * weights.variety) +
      (scores.experienceMatch * weights.experienceMatch) +
      (scores.movementType * weights.movementType) -
      (scores.injuryRisk * weights.injuryRisk)
    );
    
    return {
      exercise,
      score: totalScore
    };
  });
  
  // Sort by score and select top exercises
  scoredExercises.sort((a, b) => b.score - a.score);
  
  // Select appropriate number of exercises based on Jeff Nippard's volume recommendations
  const exerciseCount = determineExerciseCount(muscleGroup, experienceLevel);
  return scoredExercises.slice(0, exerciseCount).map(item => item.exercise);
}

3.4 Periodization Implementation
async function updatePeriodizationCycle(userId) {
  const cycle = await getPeriodizationCycle(userId);
  const user = await getUserProfile(userId);
  const workoutHistory = await getRecentWorkouts(userId, 10);
  
  // Determine if transition is needed
  let shouldTransition = false;
  let nextPhase = cycle.current_phase;
  
  // Time-based transition check
  if (
    (cycle.current_phase === 'accumulation' && cycle.phase_week >= 4) ||
    (cycle.current_phase === 'intensification' && cycle.phase_week >= 2) ||
    (cycle.current_phase === 'deload' && cycle.phase_week >= 1)
  ) {
    shouldTransition = true;
  }
  
  // Performance-based transition check
  const performanceMetrics = analyzeRecentPerformance(workoutHistory);
  if (
    cycle.current_phase === 'accumulation' && 
    performanceMetrics.plateaued
  ) {
    shouldTransition = true;
    nextPhase = 'intensification';
  }
  
  // Fatigue-based transition check
  if (performanceMetrics.fatigueScore > FATIGUE_THRESHOLD) {
    shouldTransition = true;
    nextPhase = 'deload';
  }
  
  // Apply transition if needed
  if (shouldTransition) {
    if (nextPhase === cycle.current_phase) {
      // Move to next phase in sequence
      switch (cycle.current_phase) {
        case 'accumulation':
          nextPhase = 'intensification';
          break;
        case 'intensification':
          nextPhase = 'deload';
          break;
        case 'deload':
          nextPhase = 'accumulation';
          // Increment cycle count when returning to accumulation
          await incrementCycleCount(userId);
          break;
      }
    }
    
    // Update cycle with new phase
    await updateUserPeriodizationPhase(userId, nextPhase);
    return { ...cycle, current_phase: nextPhase, phase_week: 1 };
  } else {
    // Just increment the week in current phase
    await incrementPhaseWeek(userId);
    return { ...cycle, phase_week: cycle.phase_week + 1 };
  }
}

3.5 Injury Risk Assessment
function calculateInjuryRisk(exercise, activeInjuries) {
  if (!activeInjuries || activeInjuries.length === 0) {
    return 0; // No risk if no injuries
  }
  
  let totalRisk = 0;
  
  // Get muscles involved in this exercise
  const exerciseMuscles = getExerciseMuscles(exercise.id);
  
  // For each active injury, calculate contribution to risk
  for (const injury of activeInjuries) {
    // Get affected muscles from injury body part
    const affectedMuscles = getMusclesInBodyPart(injury.body_part);
    
    // Calculate overlap between exercise muscles and affected muscles
    for (const exerciseMuscle of exerciseMuscles) {
      for (const affectedMuscle of affectedMuscles) {
        if (exerciseMuscle.muscle_id === affectedMuscle.id) {
          // Primary muscles have higher risk
          const primaryFactor = exerciseMuscle.is_primary ? 1.0 : 0.6;
          
          // Calculate risk contribution
          const riskContribution = 
            injury.pain_level * 
            exerciseMuscle.intensity * 
            primaryFactor;
            
          totalRisk += riskContribution;
        }
      }
    }
    
    // Check for joint stress
    const jointStress = calculateJointStress(exercise, injury.body_part);
    totalRisk += jointStress * injury.pain_level * 1.2;
  }
  
  return totalRisk;
}

const WorkoutExecution = ({ workout, onComplete }) => {
  const [currentExerciseIndex, setCurrentExerciseIndex] = useState(0);
  const [setLogs, setSetLogs] = useState([]);
  const [restTimer, setRestTimer] = useState(0);
  const [isResting, setIsResting] = useState(false);
  
  const currentExercise = workout.exercises[currentExerciseIndex];
  
  // Handle set completion
  const completeSet = (weight, reps, feedback) => {
    // Log the completed set
    const newLog = {
      exercise_id: currentExercise.exercise_id,
      set_number: setLogs.filter(log => log.exercise_id === currentExercise.exercise_id).length + 1,
      weight_used: weight,
      reps_completed: reps,
      feedback
    };
    setSetLogs([...setLogs, newLog]);
    
    // Check if exercise is complete
    if (newLog.set_number >= currentExercise.sets_target) {
      // Move to next exercise if available
      if (currentExerciseIndex < workout.exercises.length - 1) {
        setCurrentExerciseIndex(currentExerciseIndex + 1);
      } else {
        // Workout complete
        onComplete(setLogs);
      }
    } else {
      // Start rest timer for next set
      startRestTimer(currentExercise.rest_seconds);
    }
  };
  
  // Substitute exercise
  const substituteExercise = async () => {
    // Request a substitute exercise from the API
    const substitute = await getSubstituteExercise(
      currentExercise.exercise_id, 
      workout.user_id
    );
    
    // Replace the current exercise
    const updatedExercises = [...workout.exercises];
    updatedExercises[currentExerciseIndex] = substitute;
    
    // Update the workout
    onWorkoutUpdate({
      ...workout,
      exercises: updatedExercises
    });
  };
  
  return (
    <View style={styles.container}>
      {/* Exercise information */}
      <ExerciseCard 
        exercise={currentExercise}
        onSubstitute={substituteExercise}
      />
      
      {/* Rest timer (shown during rest) */}
      {isResting ? (
        <RestTimer 
          seconds={restTimer}
          onComplete={() => setIsResting(false)}
        />
      ) : (
        /* Set logging interface (shown during active set) */
        <SetLogger
          exercise={currentExercise}
          setNumber={setLogs.filter(log => log.exercise_id === currentExercise.exercise_id).length + 1}
          previousWeight={getPreviousWeight(setLogs, currentExercise.exercise_id)}
          targetReps={currentExercise.reps_target}
          onComplete={completeSet}
        />
      )}
      
      {/* Progress indicator */}
      <WorkoutProgress 
        currentExerciseIndex={currentExerciseIndex}
        totalExercises={workout.exercises.length}
        completedSets={setLogs.length}
        totalSets={calculateTotalSets(workout.exercises)}
      />
    </View>
  );
};

6.2 Sync Service
class SyncService {
  // Queue an API call for later execution
  async queueSync(endpoint, method, payload) {
    const id = uuid.v4();
    await db.execute(
      'INSERT INTO sync_queue (id, endpoint, method, payload, created_at) VALUES (?, ?, ?, ?, ?)',
      [id, endpoint, method, JSON.stringify(payload), new Date().toISOString()]
    );
    
    // Try to sync immediately if online
    if (await this.isOnline()) {
      this.processSyncQueue();
    }
  }
  
  // Process all pending sync items
  async processSyncQueue() {
    // Get all pending items
    const queue = await db.query('SELECT * FROM sync_queue ORDER BY created_at ASC');
    
    for (const item of queue) {
      try {
        // Execute the API call
        await api.request({
          url: item.endpoint,
          method: item.method,
          data: JSON.parse(item.payload)
        });
        
        // Remove from queue on success
        await db.execute('DELETE FROM sync_queue WHERE id = ?', [item.id]);
        
        // Update local data as synced
        if (item.endpoint.includes('workout_logs')) {
          await db.execute(
            'UPDATE local_workout_logs SET is_synced = 1 WHERE id = ?',
            [JSON.parse(item.payload).id]
          );
        } else if (item.endpoint.includes('exercise_logs')) {
          await db.execute(
            'UPDATE local_exercise_logs SET is_synced = 1 WHERE id = ?',
            [JSON.parse(item.payload).id]
          );
        }
      } catch (error) {
        // Increment attempt count
        await db.execute(
          'UPDATE sync_queue SET attempt_count = attempt_count + 1 WHERE id = ?',
          [item.id]
        );
        
        // If too many attempts, flag for manual resolution
        if (item.attempt_count >= 5) {
          await db.execute(
            'UPDATE sync_queue SET needs_resolution = 1 WHERE id = ?',
            [item.id]
          );
        }
      }
    }
  }
  
  // Listen for online status changes
  setupListeners() {
    NetInfo.addEventListener(state => {
      if (state.isConnected) {
        this.processSyncQueue();
      }
    });
  }
}

const login = async (email, password) => {
  try {
    // Authenticate with Supabase

      email,

// Token refresh mechanism
const setupTokenRefresh = (expiresAt) => {
  const expiryTime = new Date(expiresAt).getTime();
  const currentTime = new Date().getTime();
  
  // Schedule refresh for 5 minutes before expiration
  const timeToRefresh = expiryTime - currentTime - (5 * 60 * 1000);
  
  setTimeout(async () => {
    const refreshToken = await secureStorage.getItem('refresh_token');
    
    if (refreshToken) {
      try {
        const { data, error } = await supabase.auth.refreshSession({
          refresh_token: refreshToken
        });
        
        if (error) throw error;
        
        // Update stored tokens
        await secureStorage.setItem('access_token', data.session.access_token);
        await secureStorage.setItem('refresh_token', data.session.refresh_token);
        
        // Set up next refresh
        setupTokenRefresh(data.session.expires_at);
      } catch (error) {
        console.error('Token refresh error:', error);
        // Force re-login if refresh fails
        logoutAndRedirect();
      }
    }
  }, timeToRefresh);
};

const encryptData = (data) => {
  // Generate a secure key for this data item
  const key = CryptoJS.lib.WordArray.random(16).toString();
  
  // Encrypt the data
  const encrypted = CryptoJS.AES.encrypt(JSON.stringify(data), key).toString();

  return encrypted;
};

// Decrypt data when retrieved
const decryptData = async (encryptedData, dataId) => {
  // Retrieve the key
  const key = await secureStorage.getItem(`key_${dataId}`);
  
  if (!key) {

  }
  
  // Decrypt the data
  const decrypted = CryptoJS.AES.decrypt(encryptedData, key).toString(CryptoJS.enc.Utf8);
  
  return JSON.parse(decrypted);
};

8.2 Query Optimization
// Efficient data loading with pagination and prefetching
const WorkoutHistory = () => {
  const [workouts, setWorkouts] = useState([]);
  const [loading, setLoading] = useState(false);
  const [page, setPage] = useState(1);
  const [hasMore, setHasMore] = useState(true);
  
  // Load initial workouts
  useEffect(() => {
    loadWorkouts(1);
  }, []);
  
  // Load workouts with pagination
  const loadWorkouts = async (pageNum) => {
    if (loading) return;
    
    setLoading(true);
    try {
      // Fetch data with pagination
      const data = await api.getWorkoutHistory({
        page: pageNum,
        limit: 10
      });
      
      if (pageNum === 1) {
        setWorkouts(data.workouts);
      } else {
        setWorkouts([...workouts, ...data.workouts]);
      }
      
      setHasMore(data.has_more);
      setPage(pageNum);
      
      // Prefetch next page if available
      if (data.has_more) {
        api.prefetchWorkoutHistory({
          page: pageNum + 1,
          limit: 10
        });
      }
    } catch (error) {
      console.error('Error loading workouts:', error);
    } finally {
      setLoading(false);
    }
  };
  
  // Load more when user reaches the end
  const handleLoadMore = () => {
    if (hasMore && !loading) {
      loadWorkouts(page + 1);
    }
  };
  
  return (
    <FlatList
      data={workouts}
      renderItem={renderWorkout}
      keyExtractor={item => item.id}
      onEndReached={handleLoadMore}
      onEndReachedThreshold={0.5}
      ListFooterComponent={loading ? <ActivityIndicator /> : null}
    />
  );
};

Hypertrophy Essentials (Ordered by Importance)
Consistent and Adequate Sleep
7–9 hours nightly, focusing on sleep quality.

Proper Nutrition
0.8–1g protein per lb of body weight daily.
Slight caloric surplus (~250 calories above maintenance per day) to support muscle growth

Structured Gym Training Program
Training frequency: hitting each muscle group 2x/week (2 body parts/day, 3–4 days/week).
Progressive overload (gradually increasing weight, reps, or total volume).
Strict technique emphasizing safety and muscle activation.

Training Volume & Intensity
10–20 total weekly sets per muscle group.
Majority of working sets close to muscular failure (within 1–3 reps of failure).

Exercise Selection & Variation
Compound movements as a foundation (squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press).
Isolation movements as needed to support weak areas or imbalances.
Regular exercise rotation every 4–8 weeks to prevent stagnation.

Periodization & Planned De-load Weeks
Structured variation of training volume/intensity over weeks or months.
Regular de-load weeks (every 6–10 weeks) to support long-term recovery and avoid injury.

Mind-Muscle Connection and Rep Tempo
Controlled eccentrics (slower lowering of weight) and purposeful contractions.
Prioritizing muscle activation over sheer weight lifted.

Hydration & Electrolyte Balance
Maintaining proper fluid and electrolyte intake (especially sodium, potassium, magnesium).

Stress Management
Techniques (meditation, yoga, breathwork, relaxation strategies) to lower cortisol and enhance recovery.

Supplementation (Optional but Helpful)
Creatine monohydrate (3–5g daily).

Consistency and Patience

Long-term commitment to the above factors; progress measured over months/years, not weeks.
